Guarding Generational Wealth Since 1977

Nitadol Bank was founded in Zurich in 1977 by a consortium of European private bankers and Swiss gold merchants. From its origins as a bullion custody specialist, it has grown into one of the world's foremost institutions for private wealth management, digital asset custody, and cross-border financial services.

With offices in Zurich, London, Singapore, Dubai, New York, Tokyo, and Bogotá, Nitadol operates at the intersection of old-world banking discipline and modern financial technology.

Our client base spans ultra-high-net-worth individuals, family offices, sovereign wealth funds, and institutional investors across 80+ countries — including extensive operations across Africa, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open a Nitadol Private Vault account?

Click "Open Private Vault" and complete our two-step KYC registration. You'll need valid ID, proof of address, and source of funds documentation. Account activation typically takes 1–2 business days.

What currencies and assets does Nitadol support?

We support EUR, USD, CHF, GBP, AED, SGD, NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR, COP, MXN, BRL, INR, and 140+ additional fiat currencies. For digital assets: BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, and regulated tokenised assets.

Is my money safe at Nitadol Bank?

Your funds are held in fully segregated accounts and never used for proprietary trading. Digital assets are insured up to €50M per client. Nitadol is regulated by FINMA and the FCA.

What are the fees for international transfers?

Standard accounts: €8 flat fee per international wire. Gold Tier clients: zero transfer fees and zero FX spread. Domestic transfers are always free for all tiers.

Can I hold both EUR and local currency?

Yes. When you register, you may select your primary account currency (EUR or USD). If your country uses a different local currency, Nitadol automatically provisions a secondary local currency account — complete with a country-specific IBAN. Both balances are visible on your dashboard.

How do currency conversions work between my accounts?

When you transfer between your primary (EUR/USD) account and your local currency account, the system applies the live mid-market exchange rate. The converted amount is instantly reflected in both account balances.

Can I send money to other banks or IBANs?

Yes. You can send to any valid IBAN worldwide via SWIFT. Transfers to other Nitadol clients are recognised automatically and credited instantly. External SWIFT transfers typically settle in 1–2 business days.

What happens if I try to send money with insufficient funds?

If your account balance is zero or below the transfer amount, the transaction will be rejected immediately with an "Insufficient Funds" notification. You will be prompted to top up your account before retrying.

Is Nitadol Bank available in Colombia and Latin America?

Yes! Nitadol serves clients across Colombia (COP), Brazil (BRL), Mexico (MXN), Argentina (ARS), Chile (CLP), Peru (PEN), and 10 other Latin American countries. Our Bogotá regional office provides dedicated support for LatAm clients.
